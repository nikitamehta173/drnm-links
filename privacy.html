Joining my newsletter

When you sign up, the form is run by Flodesk, my email provider. I keep your email address (and your name, if you give it) so I can send you my newsletter.

  • Why I'm allowed to: you've given your consent by signing up.
  • How long: until you unsubscribe. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or you can email me.
  • Who else sees it: Flodesk stores it for me. Flodesk is based in the United States, so your details may be held there. See Flodesk's privacy policy. I never sell or share your details with anyone else.
